    fd11_be_mispredict: Backend Mispredict¶

    Open waveform

    Runs Surfer in the browser, with nothing to install. The signal setup takes a few seconds to apply.

    1. Introduction¶

    fd11_be_mispredict (FD-11) runs a conditional with a pseudo-random outcome, then a call and its return, in a loop of 32 passes.

    The conditional's outcome has no pattern for the predictors to learn, so the backend often resolves it against the prediction and redirects. In the redirect cycle, the FTQ squashes the younger entries and flushes the IFU, the history rolls back and takes the corrected outcome, and the RAS restores its pointers from the entry's snapshot. Fetch then restarts at the corrected PC.

    The backend raises 21 mispredict redirects in the run, and the first fetch after each one starts at the redirect PC. The result line in the log file reports those counts.

    This scenario runs in the 1-prediction slot Pacino configuration, NUM_PRED_SLOTS=1.

    2. Run the scenario¶

    Run simulation: 

    make -C examples/fe_demo fd11_be_mispredict

    View the waveform (runs simulation first if FST is not up to date): 

    make -C examples/fe_demo view S=fd11_be_mispredict

    Console output: 

    -I: fd11_be_mispredict : PASS : cyc = 480

    Log file output: 

    -I: fd11_be_mispredict: PASS, backend mispredict redirects 21,
    fetches at the redirect address 21, history rollbacks 82, RAS restores 82,
    events checked 18 of 18, cyc 480

    The testbench monitor (examples/fe_demo/tb/fe_demo_mon.svh) supplies the counts in the log line:

    • backend mispredict redirects
      • n_be_misp
      • cycles with tb.bkend_ftq_redir_val asserted and tb.bkend_ftq_redir_cause RC_MISPREDICT
    • fetches at the redirect address
      • n_be_fix
      • redirects whose first following fetch, with ftq_ifu_req_val and ftq_ifu_req_rdy asserted and no flush, has ftq_ifu_start_pc equal to the redirect PC
    • history rollbacks
      • n_rollback
      • cycles with ftq_rollback_val asserted, for a redirect from any source
    • RAS restores
      • n_ras_rest
      • cycles with ras_restore_val asserted, the same net as ftq_rollback_val

    3. The program¶

    The program is written by the task prog_fd11 in programs/fd11_be_mispredict.svh. The code consists of a short prologue, then a loop of 32 passes starting at top, and a short epilogue.

    Each pass runs the conditional A, then a call to fn from 0x8000_0010, and the return to 0x8000_0014. The back edge at 0x8000_0016 returns to top. The back edge is taken on passes 0 to 30.

    The program runs in machine mode with translation off, the addresses are physical. The program starts at the reset vector, RESET_VECTOR = 0x8000_0000 (bp_defines_pkg.sv).

                                                             Position from

                                                         Position from 0x400

0x8000_0402         addi   t0, t0, 1      ; filler       1
0x8000_0406         ret                   ; return       3

    A position is the halfword offset of an instruction from the start of its prediction block. The 32-bit instructions take two positions. The four position columns at the top are the block starts that hold these instructions in the run.

    The c.addi and addi instructions do no useful work. Their purpose is to mix 16-bit and 32-bit instructions and to separate the branches.

    The direction of A is one bit per pass from the program writer's 16-bit Galois LFSR, with taps 0xB400 and seed 0xACE1 (lfsr_bit in rtl/core/frontend/fe_top/tb/models/fe_tb_writer.svh). From pass 0 to pass 31 the bits are:

    1000 0111 0010 0011 0100 0110 1101 1100

    A bit of 1 is taken, which skips the c.addi at 0x8000_000E. A is taken on 15 passes and not taken on 17. The direction is resolved by the modeled backend, and the register operands play no part in it. The code is the same on every pass, and only the expected instruction stream changes.

    4. Walkthrough¶

    Cycle numbers below are events in section 5; each event is a marker in the waveform. A signal the testbench drives, such as tb.bkend_ftq_redir_val, changes one time unit after the clock edge, so a backend redirect reads at the marker of the next cycle.

    4.1 The backend redirect¶

    The backend model retires the bundle against the expected instruction stream. When the instruction after a control transfer is not the expected one, it raises tb.bkend_ftq_redir_val with cause RC_MISPREDICT, the FTQ index and position of the transfer, the resolved direction and the correct PC.1

    In the FTQ's p0 arbiter a backend redirect outranks every other redirect, without comparison.2 In the redirect cycle:

    • ftq_pred_pc_p0 is the redirect PC, and ftq_pred_idx_p0 is the entry after the named one. The younger entries are squashed.
    • ftq_ifu_flush_val is asserted, naming that same entry.3
    • ftq_rollback_val is asserted, with ftq_rollback_corr.4
    • ras_restore_val is asserted, with the named entry's RAS snapshot.5

    The run has 21 backend redirects, and all have cause RC_MISPREDICT and tb.bkend_ftq_redir_self deasserted.

    All 21 first fetches start at the redirect PC. The p0 request is held in every redirect cycle by the FTB update scheduler, so ftq_pred_val_p0 is deasserted while ftq_pred_pc_p0 shows the redirect PC.6 The request becomes valid 1 or 2 cycles after cyc, and the first fetch is 3 to 5 cycles after cyc.

    4.2 The branches the backend redirects¶

    A's first redirect is at cyc 20 (event 2), at position 5 of the block at 0x8000_0000, before any predictor holds it. Fetch restarts at 0x8000_0010 at cyc 23 (event 3). The return and the back edge are first redirected at cyc 49 (event 5) and cyc 60 (event 7), and fetch restarts at 0x8000_0014 at cyc 53 (event 6).

    A is redirected in 18 of the 32 passes, 8 times to 0x8000_0010 and 10 times to 0x8000_000E. No backend redirect names A in passes 1, 3, 4, 7, 14, 15, 16, 17, 22, 25, 26, 28, 30 and 31. The first redirect of A not taken is at cyc 86 in pass 2 (event 12), and fetch restarts at 0x8000_000E at cyc 89 (event 13).

    The FTB update at cyc 82 (event 10) records the call at position 4 of the block at top. A already holds the entry's one slot, so the FTB splits the entry at the call, with upd_cut_pos 4, and the block at top now ends at 0x8000_0010. From then on, both predicted directions of A continue at the block at 0x8000_0010. A predicted taken ends the fetch at A, and A predicted not taken also fetches the c.addi at 0x8000_000E. The block at 0x8000_000E is used only after a backend redirect to it.

    The back edge is not redirected again until it falls through in pass 31, at cyc 471 (event 15), and fetch restarts at 0x8000_001A at cyc 474 (event 16). The return is not redirected again at all.

    4.3 The other redirects¶

    The 82 rollbacks in the log line are the redirects the FTQ takes from all sources, up to cyc 480.

    Source      Count   Target    Branch
backend     21                section 4.1
predecode   3       0x400     the call
p3          2       0x010     A
p2          56      0x014     the return

    The predecode redirects are for the call, once in each block start that holds it. These are position 0 of the block at 0x8000_0010 at cyc 29 (event 4), position 4 of the block at 0x8000_0008 at cyc 69, and position 1 of the block at 0x8000_000E at cyc 95. A predecode redirect is also asserted at cyc 19 and cyc 59, so the marker of cyc 19 shows the rollback of event 1 with the predecode redirect's values. The backend redirect rises one time unit later in both cycles and wins.

    The p3 redirects are at cyc 76 (event 9) and cyc 83, and both take A to 0x8000_0010. The p2 redirects start at cyc 72 (event 8). A uBTB return slot is taken at p1 only when the stack read at p1 is valid.7 On 57 of the 61 p1 slots accepted for the block at 0x8000_0400 it is not valid, so the slot is not taken and p2 redirects to 0x8000_0014.

    4.4 The history rollback¶

    bp_history moves the pointer back to the first bit of the entry the rollback names, writes the corrected outcome bits, advances past them, and records the corrected bundle in that entry's checkpoint.8 Each backend redirect in this run writes one bit, the resolved direction of the branch.

    In the first three redirects the pointer moves forward by the one corrected bit, because no younger entry has advanced it. The rollback at cyc 86 is the first to move it back. The speculative stream has advanced bpu_ghist_ptr to 10, and rolling back to FTQ index 7 leaves it at 6 at cyc 86.

    The backend model checks the corrected bit in the cycle after a redirect of a conditional: the history bit before the pointer must equal the resolved direction.9 Its counters tb.n_hist_ok and tb.n_hist_bad end at 20 and 0, for 18 redirects of A and 2 of the back edge. The return's redirect is not checked.

    4.5 The RAS restore¶

    The restore is pointer-only. TOSR, TOSW and BOS load from the entry's snapshot, and the stack data is not cleared.10

    ras_restore_val is asserted for all 82 redirects, and bp_cluster drops the restore when the redirect is the block's own p2 or p3 redirect.11 That leaves 24 restores that reach the RAS, from the 21 backend redirects and the 3 predecode redirects.

    The first restore to move a pointer is at cyc 86 (event 11), where TOSW goes from 2 to 0. The first to move BOS is at cyc 125 (event 14), where TOSR, TOSW and BOS go from 4/5/2 to 1/3/1. In all 17 redirects of A after cyc 19, TOSW moves back. The pointers wrap at 16 entries, as the rows from cyc 298 show. After pass 0, the backend redirects no return.

    4.6 The redirect in the waveform¶

    The redirect at cyc 86 shows each part. The waveform group backend shows the redirect, bp_cluster_p0 the p0 request, ftq_ifu the flush and the restart, and bp_history_ras the rollback and the restore.

    At cyc 85, bpu_ghist_ptr is 10 and u_bpu.u_ras.tosw is 2.

    At cyc 86 (events 11 and 12), tb.bkend_ftq_redir_val is asserted with tb.bkend_ftq_redir_idx 7, tb.bkend_ftq_redir_pos 1, tb.bkend_ftq_redir_pc 0x8000_000E and tb.bkend_ftq_redir_taken 0. ftq_pred_pc_p0 is 0x8000_000E and ftq_pred_idx_p0 is 8, while u_ftq.u_npc.w_hold holds the request. ftq_ifu_flush_val is asserted with ftq_ifu_flush_idx 8. ftq_rollback_val and ftq_rollback_corr are asserted with ftq_rollback_idx 7. bpu_ghist_ptr is 6 and u_bpu.u_ras.tosw is 0, because these registers load at the clock edge at this marker. One time unit later the redirect falls, and ftq_pred_val_p0 is asserted with ftq_pred_pc_p0 0x8000_000E.

    At cyc 89 (event 13), ftq_ifu_req_val and ftq_ifu_req_rdy are asserted with ftq_ifu_idx 8 and ftq_ifu_start_pc 0x8000_000E.
