    fd23_jump_cut: A Jump Behind Two Conditionals¶

    Open waveform

    Runs Surfer in the browser, with nothing to install. The signal setup takes a few seconds to apply.

    1. What the scenario shows¶

    fd23_jump_cut is the FD-23 scenario of the front-end demonstration. A fetch block holds two conditional branches followed by a jump. With two prediction slots an FTB entry holds at most two branches, so when the jump resolves in that block the FTB splits the entry: it keeps the two conditionals and ends the block at the jump's address (planning/arch/ftb_decisions.md section 3, BP-140). When both conditionals are predicted not taken, the block ends at the jump by the FTB's own stored end, and the next block, which starts at the jump, predicts the jump in slot 0. The scenario runs on the two-slot model (SCEN_2S in the Makefile). demo_explainer.md describes how a scenario is built and run, and defines the terms used here.

    DOC-006 wrote this scenario for the cluster's cut (planning/arch/fe_decisions.md section 10, BP-138 and BP-139), which ended such a block at the jump when the FTB entry held all three branches. Since BP-140 no entry the FTB writes holds a branch that no slot carries, the cut does not fire, and DOC-007 re-pointed the scenario at the split.

    When the scenario passes, it prints one line. This is the line from a run on 2026-10-19, the run the cyc values below come from:

    -I: fd23_jump_cut: PASS, blocks the FTB ends at the jump 23,
    the jump predicted in slot 0 of the next block 21,
    blocks cut by the cluster 0, cyc 244

    The monitor, tb/fe_demo_mon.svh lines 602 to 628, counts in the p2 groups the FTQ accepted (tb.dut.u_ftq.w_ok_slot_p2):

    • blocks the FTB ends at the jump, the monitor variable n_jmp_end: a group whose two slots are conditionals predicted not taken, whose block end, tb.dut.bpu_blk_pft_p2, is the jump's address, and in which the cluster's cut, tb.dut.u_bpu.w_cut_p2, is clear. The jump's address is the testbench variable jmp_pc (tb/fe_demo_mon.svh line 153). The program sets it when it writes the jump, so the end is checked against the program;
    • the jump predicted in slot 0 of the next block, n_jmp_next: the next accepted group whose block starts at that address, tb.dut.u_bpu.r_pc_p2, with a jump in slot 0 at position 0, predicted taken;
    • blocks cut by the cluster, n_cut: accepted groups with w_cut_p2 set, reported for information.

    The scenario passes when the first two counts are greater than zero (tb/fe_demo_proofs.svh line 180) and every instruction the testbench expects retires without error. On the one-slot model the counts are 0 and the scenario fails. The run took 244 cycles.

    2. The program¶

    The program is written by the task prog_fd23 in programs/fd23_jump_cut.svh. Between a short prologue and epilogue it runs a loop of 24 passes. The loop's first block holds the conditionals C1 and C2, never taken, followed by the jump J to a block at 0x8000_0400 that holds the back edge. The program runs in machine mode with translation off, so the addresses are physical. It starts at the reset vector, RESET_VECTOR = 0x8000_0000 (bp_defines_pkg.sv). The loop's first instruction, top, is at 0x8000_0008.

    Address      Label  Instruction              Purpose
0x8000_0000         c.addi a0, 1             prologue filler
0x8000_0002         addi t0, t0, 1           prologue filler
0x8000_0006         c.addi a0, 1             prologue filler
0x8000_0008  top:   c.addi a0, 1             loop body filler
0x8000_000A  C1:    bne x6, x0, 0x8000_0010  never taken
0x8000_000E         c.addi a0, 1             always executed
0x8000_0010  C2:    bne x6, x0, 0x8000_0016  never taken
0x8000_0014         c.addi a0, 1             always executed
0x8000_0016  J:     jal x0, 0x8000_0400      the jump
0x8000_0400         c.addi a0, 1             filler
0x8000_0402         addi t0, t0, 1           filler
0x8000_0406         bne x6, x0, 0x8000_0008  back edge to top
0x8000_040A         c.addi a0, 1             epilogue filler
0x8000_040C         addi t0, t0, 1           epilogue filler
0x8000_0410         c.addi a0, 1             epilogue filler
0x8000_0412         jal x0, 0                jump to itself; the run
                                             ends here

    In the block that starts at top, C1 is at halfword position 1, C2 at position 4 and J at position 7. The back edge is taken on passes 0 to 22 and falls through on pass 23. The program writer writes the loop body once per pass at the same addresses, so the code in memory does not change (demo_explainer.md, section 2); x6 is never read. Before it writes J, the program sets jmp_pc to J's address, 0x8000_0016.

    3. Walkthrough¶

    3.1 The FTB entry for top¶

    The FTB keeps one entry per block start. An update for a branch resolved in a block from that start adds the branch to the entry. When the entry already holds a branch at that position, the update changes that branch in place. The entry stores its branches in position order. An entry holds at most NUM_PRED_SLOTS branches. When an update would make it hold more, the lowest NUM_PRED_SLOTS positions are kept, the block's end becomes the address of the lowest branch not kept, and a jump not kept clears the jump field. This is the split (rtl/core/frontend/bpu/rtl/ftb_cntrl.sv, THE UPDATE, lines 464 to 494; the split at lines 592 to 615). The uBTB applies the same rule (apply_upd, ubtb.sv lines 291 to 376).

    C1 and C2 resolve first: at cyc 52 and 53 the FTB allocates the entry for top with C1, then adds C2. The entry ends one block above its start, at 0x8000_0028. At cyc 56 the first p2 group for top predicts both taken (TAGE's base table starts weakly taken), and the statistical corrector reverses them at p3, at cyc 57, to the block's end. J then resolves in a block from top and is the third branch: at cyc 65, in the ftb_update group, tb.dut.ftb_upd_pc_u0 is 0x8000_0008, ftb_upd_pos_u0 7, ftb_upd_is_jmp_u0 1, and tb.dut.u_bpu.u_ftb.u_ftb_cntrl.upd_split is 1 with upd_cut_pos 7. The entry keeps C1 and C2 and now ends at position 7, J's address.

    3.2 The block ends at the jump¶

    At p2 the cluster places each conditional field of the entry in its slot and the jump field in the lowest slot that holds no conditional (p2_class_comb, rtl/core/frontend/bpu/rtl/bp_cluster.sv lines 1010 to 1046). The entry for top has no jump field, so the two slots hold C1 and C2. The block's fall-through, the address fetch continues at when no slot is taken, is the FTB's stored end when the FTB answered, no branch is cut and no jump is held (w_pft_p2, p2_succ_comb, lines 1133 to 1141); it leaves the cluster as bpu_blk_pft_p2 (line 1275). The cut, which would end the block at a branch the FTB reports and no slot carries (w_cut_p2, lines 1064 to 1074), stays clear.

    In the waveform, the first accepted p2 group of the block at top that ends at J is at cyc 71. In the bp_cluster_p2_ftb group, tb.dut.u_bpu.r_pc_p2 is 0x8000_0008. The FTB answer holds both conditional fields (w_ftb_br_valid_p2.[0] and .[1]) and no jump (w_ftb_jmp_valid_p2 0), w_cut_p2 is 0, and tb.dut.bpu_blk_pft_p2 is 0x8000_0016, J's address. In the bp_cluster_p2 group, tb.dut.bpu_slot_p2.[0] is C1 and .[1] is C2, both COND at pos 1 and 4, not taken, pred_src PRED_TAGE. The uBTB was split the same way: its p1 prediction for top, tb.dut.bpu_pred_pft_p1, is 0x8000_0016 from cyc 70, so p1 and p2 agree.

    3.3 The next block predicts the jump¶

    The next block starts at 0x8000_0016. The predictors have not seen this block start, so at first neither the uBTB nor the FTB answers for it. Fetch runs past J, and predecode at F3 finds it (predecode redirects to 0x8000_0400 at cyc 79 and 92). The update for J from that start allocates its entry at cyc 91, with J at position 0. From then on the FTB answers for 0x8000_0016 with J in its jump field, and the cluster places J in slot 0.

    The first counted pair is at cyc 97 and 98. At cyc 97 the block at top ends at 0x8000_0016, as in 3.2; at cyc 98, r_pc_p2 is 0x8000_0016 and tb.dut.bpu_slot_p2.[0] is DIRECT_UNC at pos 0, taken, with target 0x8000_0400 and pred_src PRED_FTB. The two blocks counted at cyc 71 and 84 ended at J before J's own entry existed; their next groups had no FTB answer, so the count of blocks ended at J, 23, exceeds the count of next blocks, 21.

    RTL: the split is upd_split and upd_cut_pos in ftb_cntrl.sv; the block end is w_pft_p2 in bp_cluster.sv; the monitor is tb/fe_demo_mon.svh lines 602 to 628.
