    fd05_p3_override: p3 Override¶

    Open waveform

    Runs Surfer in the browser, with nothing to install. The signal setup takes a few seconds to apply.

    1. Introduction¶

    fd05_p3_override (FD-5) runs a 96-pass loop around two conditional branches, A and B, whose directions follow short periodic patterns: A is taken on odd passes, and B on every third pass.

    At p3 the statistical corrector (SC) can disagree with the direction TAGE published at p2. When it does, the p3 successor differs from the p2 successor and the BPU issues a p3 redirect, with the slot's pred_src set to PRED_SC.

    The FTQ accepts 33 p3 redirects, between cyc 47 and cyc 822. All 33 are SC reversals of a TAGE direction. The result line in the log file reports both counts.

    This scenario runs in the 1-prediction slot Pacino configuration, NUM_PRED_SLOTS=1. FD-22 (fd22_sc_slot1) shows SC reversing the second slot of a prediction block, in the 2-prediction slot configuration.

    2. Run the scenario¶

    Run simulation: 

    make -C examples/fe_demo fd05_p3_override

    View the waveform (runs simulation first if FST is not up to date): 

    make -C examples/fe_demo view S=fd05_p3_override

    Console output: 

    -I: fd05_p3_override : PASS : cyc = 975

    Log file output: 

    -I: fd05_p3_override: PASS, p3 redirects 33, SC reversals among them 33,
    events checked 15 of 15, cyc 975

    The testbench monitor, tb/fe_demo_mon.svh, supplies the counts in the log line:

    • p3 redirects
      • n_redir_p3 in tb/fe_demo_mon.svh
      • cycles in which the FTQ accepted a p3 redirect
    • SC reversals among them
      • n_redir_p3_sc in tb/fe_demo_mon.svh
      • those p3 redirects whose redirecting slot has pred_src PRED_SC

    3. The program¶

    The program is written by the task prog_fd05 in programs/fd05_p3_override.svh. The code consists of a short prologue, then a 96-pass loop starting at top, and a short epilogue.

    The loop holds three conditional branches. A at 0x8000_000E is taken on odd passes, and skips to At. B at 0x8000_001A is taken on every third pass, and skips to Bt. The back edge BE at 0x8000_0026 returns to top.

    The program runs in machine mode with translation off, so the addresses are physical. The program starts at the reset vector, RESET_VECTOR = 0x8000_0000 (bp_defines_pkg.sv).

                                                                   Position
Address      Label   Instruction          Purpose              from top
0x8000_0000          c.addi a0, 1         ; prologue
0x8000_0002          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; prologue
0x8000_0006          c.addi a0, 1         ; prologue

0x8000_0008  top:    c.addi a0, 1         ; filler                0
0x8000_000A          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; filler                1
0x8000_000E  A:      bne    x6, x0, At    ; taken on odd passes   3
0x8000_0012          c.addi a0, 1         ; skipped if A taken    5
0x8000_0014          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; skipped if A taken    6
0x8000_0018  At:     c.addi a0, 1         ; filler                8
0x8000_001A  B:      bne    x6, x0, Bt    ; every third pass      9
0x8000_001E          c.addi a0, 1         ; skipped if B taken   11
0x8000_0020          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; skipped if B taken   12
0x8000_0024  Bt:     c.addi a0, 1         ; filler               14
0x8000_0026  BE:     bne    x6, x0, top   ; back edge            15

0x8000_002A          c.addi a0, 1         ; epilogue
0x8000_002C          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; epilogue
0x8000_0030          c.addi a0, 1         ; epilogue
0x8000_0032  hold:   jal    x0, hold      ; termination

    A position is the halfword offset of an instruction from the start of its prediction block. The column gives positions from top, so A is at 3, B at 9 and BE at 15. The 32-bit instructions take two positions.

    With one prediction slot, an FTB entry holds one branch, so the loop is predicted as several blocks. Where a block starts depends on the path taken and on which branches have been resolved. The accepted p2 slot groups, by block:

    Block start Reached by Branch Position Block end Groups
    0x8000_0008 (top) The back edge taken A 3 0x8000_001A after the split 106
    0x8000_0008 (top) The back edge taken A 3 0x8000_0028 before the split 23
    0x8000_0012 A corrected to not taken B 4 0x8000_0032 8
    0x8000_0018 (At) A taken B 1 0x8000_0026 87
    0x8000_001A (B) The block at top ending at B B 0 0x8000_0026 41
    0x8000_001E B corrected to not taken BE 4 0x8000_003E 10
    0x8000_0024 (Bt) B taken BE 1 0x8000_0044 46
    0x8000_0026 (BE) A block ending at BE BE 0 0x8000_0046 78

    The same blocks are shown below against the code.

    The c.addi and addi instructions do no useful work. Their purpose is to mix 16-bit and 32-bit instructions and to separate the branches.

    The direction of each branch is chosen by the program writer class and resolved by the modeled backend. The register operands play no part in the taken/not taken state. The code is the same on every pass, and only the expected instruction stream changes.

    A is taken on 48 of the 96 passes, and B on 32, on passes where the pass number modulo 3 is 2. The back edge is taken on passes 0 to 94 and falls through on pass 95 (event 13).

    4. Walkthrough¶

    Cycle numbers below are events in section 5; each event is a marker in the waveform.

    4.1 p3 redirects from SC reversals¶

    SC responds at p3 when w_sc_pred_rdy_p3 is asserted, SC is enabled, and the response's branch_id matches the entry at p3.1 For a conditional branch with an SC response, and no direction from the loop predictor at p2, the p3 direction is sc_pred_tkn. Otherwise the p3 direction stays the p2 direction.2

    The BPU compares the p3 successor with the p2 successor and issues a p3 redirect when they differ.3 With one prediction slot, the successor changes only when SC changes the direction, so every p3 redirect in this scenario is an SC reversal, which is why the two counts in the result line agree. The p3 slot records the reversal: pred_src becomes PRED_SC when taken differs from the p2 direction.4 The FTQ accepts the redirect and takes its PC as the next p0 request, ahead of any p2 redirect.5

    The FTQ accepts 33 p3 redirects, shown below by block. In all 33 the reversed direction came from TAGE, pred_src PRED_TAGE at p2. The last three columns give what happens to the FTQ entry afterward: it commits, so SC was right; the backend redirects at it, so SC was wrong; or a redirect at an older entry squashes it first, so the branch is never resolved.

                                 p3                       SC     SC     not
block        branch  pos     direction  target count  right  wrong  resolved
0x8000_0008  A       3       not taken  ..001A   17    11      0      6
0x8000_0008  A       3       not taken  ..0028    2     0      1      1
0x8000_0008  A       3       taken      ..0018    2     1      0      1
0x8000_0012  B       4       taken      ..0024    2     1      1      0
0x8000_0018  B       1       taken      ..0024    7     0      3      4
0x8000_001A  B       0       taken      ..0024    3     0      3      0

    Of the 21 reversals that resolve, SC is right on 13 and wrong on 8. The two branches differ: 12 of A's 13 are right, and 7 of B's 8 are wrong. Every reversal of B is to taken, and B is taken on only one pass in three. The backend first corrects a reversal at cyc 93 (event 4), and the first reversal that commits is at cyc 201 (event 6). The last reversal of B is at cyc 505 (event 10). The backend reports 37 mispredictions in the run. The last one in the loop is at cyc 684 (event 11), and reversals of A continue after it.

    The reversals across the run are shown below.

    A reversal of A to not taken redirects to the end of the block at top. That end is top + 0x20, 0x8000_0028, until the FTB update for B at position 9 splits the entry at cyc 261 (event 7). The reversal at cyc 178 (event 5) is one of the two before the split. From then on the end is B's PC, 0x8000_001A, first at cyc 292 (event 8). The first p3 redirect is at cyc 47 (event 3) and the last at cyc 822 (event 12). The entries for the blocks at 0x8000_0018 and 0x8000_001A split the same way, ending at BE, at cyc 43 (event 2) and cyc 337 (event 9).

    4.2 First reversals¶

    The first SC reversal is at cyc 47 (event 3), of B in the block at 0x8000_0018. One cycle earlier, at cyc 46, group bp_cluster_p2 shows bpu_slot_p2.[0] holding B at pos 1, with taken deasserted and pred_src PRED_TAGE. In group tage_p2, tage_pred_tkn is deasserted at the same cycle.

    At cyc 47, in group sc_p3, u_bpu.w_sc_hit_p3.[0] and u_bpu.w_taken_p3.[0] are asserted, the opposite of the p2 direction. In group bp_cluster_p3, bpu_slot_p3.[0] holds B with taken asserted and pred_src PRED_SC, and bpu_redir_p3.[0].valid is asserted with target_pc 0x8000_0024, B's target. In group ftq, u_ftq.w_ok_redir_p3 and u_ftq.w_redir_val are asserted.

    The reversal of A at cyc 178 (event 5) goes the other way. At cyc 177, bpu_slot_p2.[0] holds A at pos 3 with taken asserted and pred_src PRED_TAGE. At cyc 178, u_bpu.w_taken_p3.[0] is deasserted, bpu_slot_p3.[0] has taken deasserted and pred_src PRED_SC, and target_pc is 0x8000_0028, the end of the block at top before the split.

    The same reversal at cyc 292 (event 8), after the split at cyc 261 (event 7), has target_pc 0x8000_001A, B's PC.
