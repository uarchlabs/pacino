    fd08_indirect: Indirect Jump¶

    Open waveform

    Runs Surfer in the browser, with nothing to install. The signal setup takes a few seconds to apply.

    fd08_indirect is the FD-8 demonstration scenario. It is a demo, not a verification test. Each of 40 passes runs an indirect jump and an indirect call whose targets alternate between two addresses from pass to pass. Each is preceded by a conditional whose direction alternates with it, so global history tells the two cases apart. The FTB holds one target per jump; ITTAGE learns both targets under their histories and supplies the right one at p2. The run passes if at least one retired indirect took its correct target from ITTAGE.

    The scenario runs on the one-slot model (Makefile SCEN_2S, DOC-006). A recorded run:

    -I: fd08_indirect: PASS, indirect targets predicted by ITTAGE 50, cyc 1147

    1. The program¶

    Source: programs/fd08_indirect.svh, task prog_fd08 (line 19). Addresses derived from the source (RESET_VECTOR 0x8000_0000); odd is pass number mod 2:

    top = 0x8000_0008   c.addi
top+2       A:      bne x6,x0,top+8    taken when odd; skips one c.addi
top+8       J:      jalr x0,0(x7)      to ta (odd) or tb (even)
ta = 0x8000_0400    mixed(2); jal x0,jn
tb = 0x8000_0480    mixed(1); jal x0,jn
jn = 0x8000_0500    c.addi
jn+2        C:      bne x6,x0,jn+8     taken when even
jn+8        K:      jalr x1,0(x28)     call fa (odd) or fb (even)
fa = 0x8000_0600    mixed(2); ret
fb = 0x8000_0680    mixed(1); ret
jn+12               c.addi
jn+14               bne x6,x0,jn+22    taken on the last pass (exit)
jn+18               jal x0,top         back to top on passes 0..38
    • The writer decides every outcome and target (bcc, jalr_to, rtl/core/frontend/fe_top/tb/models/fe_tb_writer.svh:412 and :389). jalr_to writes jalr rd, 0(rs1), the same encoding every pass (enc_jalr, line 345); the backend retires against the expected stream, so x7 and x28 are never read.
    • J is INDIRECT_NONRET (neither register a link) and K is INDIRECT_CALL (rd x1), by the type rule of jalr_to (fe_tb_writer.svh:396-401). The ret in fa and fb is a RETURN, predicted by the RAS.
    • exit_or_jal (fe_tb_writer.svh:615) writes the loop exit.
    • 40 passes give 80 retired indirects: J and K retire 40 times each, 20 times to each of their two targets (derived from the source).

    2. Why the program produces the behavior¶

    1. The FTB jump slot holds one target per jump (jmp.tgt, written from ftb_upd_jmp_target_u0, rtl/core/frontend/bpu/rtl/ftb_cntrl.sv:553-554). A target that changes every pass is wrong at p2 whenever the FTB target is the one used.
    2. ITTAGE takes the folded global history (folded_hist, rtl/core/frontend/bpu/rtl/ittage.sv:53-54). A and C alternate in step with the targets, so the history at J and at K differs between odd and even passes and the two targets fall in different entries.
    3. Allocation. A target mispredict allocates when the provider is not the last table and a candidate exists (alc_upd, rtl/core/frontend/bpu/rtl/ittage_cntrl.sv:832-850); the new entry holds the resolved target.
    4. Selection at p2. For an INDIRECT_NONRET or INDIRECT_CALL slot the cluster takes the ITTAGE target when ITTAGE answered for this entry with a tagged hit (it_hit, rtl/core/frontend/bpu/rtl/bp_cluster.sv:1148-1150), primary or alternate target by ittage_using_primary (lines 1151-1153), and records pred_src = PRED_ITTAGE; otherwise the FTB jump target and PRED_FTB (lines 1171-1174). ittage_hit is the primary-table hit (ittage_cntrl.sv:557).

    3. What the monitor counts¶

    At retirement (tb/fe_demo_mon.svh:311-316):

    n_itt_ok   a retired INDIRECT_NONRET or INDIRECT_CALL whose FTQ entry's
           final slot at its position is valid, has
           pred_src == PRED_ITTAGE, is taken, and has the expected
           target

    The final slot is the last group the FTQ accepted for the entry, p1 then p2 then p3 (fin_log, tb/fe_demo_mon.svh:270-279).

    The proof (tb/fe_demo_proofs.svh:83) is n_itt_ok > 0. tb_fe_demo.sv:160 also requires the whole stream to retire with no errors.

    4. Reading the numbers¶

    • 50 of the 80 retired indirects had their right target from ITTAGE. The other 30 include the first executions of each, before ITTAGE has allocated; their sources were not traced one by one. On the two-slot model the same program gives 58 (DOC-007 run).
    • A target from ITTAGE that is wrong is not counted, and neither is one that reached the entry's final slot from another predictor.
    • Departure from FD-8 in planning/showcase/fe_demo_scenarios.md: the spec's program is "an indirect jump through a table with a repeating target pattern". The build has no table: an indirect jump and an indirect call whose targets alternate, each after a conditional that alternates with it (tb_fe_top indir). The proof matches the spec.

    5. Waveform¶

    The saved view is waves/fd08_indirect.sucl, which make view S=fd08_indirect opens in Surfer. Look at:

    • w_ittage_pred_rdy_p2 with w_ittage_pred_meta_p2.[0].ittage_hit set and ittage_prm_tgt (VA[40:1]) alternating between the two targets of the slot.
    • bpu_slot_p2.[0]: on the jump's slot, pred_src PRED_ITTAGE and target 0x8000_0400 / 0x8000_0480 for J, 0x8000_0600 / 0x8000_0680 for K.
    • bpu_redir_p2.[0] with w_ok_redir_p2: a p2 redirect, the p2 target differing from the block's p1 prediction.
