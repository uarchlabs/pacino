    fd06_loop_pred: Loop Predictor¶

    Open waveform

    Runs Surfer in the browser, with nothing to install. The signal setup takes a few seconds to apply.

    1. Introduction¶

    fd06_loop_pred (FD-6) runs a 16-pass outer loop around an inner loop of 10 iterations. The inner loop's branch, I, is taken on the first 9 iterations and not taken on the tenth, which exits the inner loop.

    The loop predictor (LP) learns the inner loop's trip count, and its confidence rises each time an exit arrives at the same count. At full confidence, the LP supplies I's direction, including the not-taken exit, and the slot's pred_src is PRED_LOOP.

    The LP predicts the exits of the last 8 passes, 8 to 15, not taken. The result line in the log file reports that count.

    This scenario runs in the 1-prediction slot Pacino configuration, NUM_PRED_SLOTS=1.

    The testbench monitor, tb/fe_demo_mon.svh, supplies the count in the log line:

    3. The program¶

    The program is written by the task prog_fd06 in programs/fd06_loop_pred.svh. The code consists of a short prologue, then a 16-pass outer loop starting at otop, with a 10-iteration inner loop starting at itop, and a short epilogue.

    The inner loop's branch I at 0x8000_0016 returns to itop. The outer loop's branch O at 0x8000_0020 returns to otop.

    The program runs in machine mode with translation off, the addresses are physical. The program starts at the reset vector, RESET_VECTOR = 0x8000_0000 (bp_defines_pkg.sv).

                                                                  Position from
Address      Label   Instruction          Purpose              otop   itop
0x8000_0000          c.addi a0, 1         ; prologue
0x8000_0002          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; prologue
0x8000_0006          c.addi a0, 1         ; prologue

0x8000_0008  otop:   c.addi a0, 1         ; filler                0
0x8000_000A          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; filler                1
0x8000_000E  itop:   c.addi a0, 1         ; filler                3      0
0x8000_0010          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; filler                4      1
0x8000_0014          c.addi a0, 1         ; filler                6      3
0x8000_0016  I:      bne    x6, x0, itop  ; inner loop branch     7      4
0x8000_001A          c.addi a0, 1         ; filler                9      6
0x8000_001C          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; filler               10      7
0x8000_0020  O:      bne    x6, x0, otop  ; outer loop branch    12      9

0x8000_0024          c.addi a0, 1         ; epilogue
0x8000_0026          addi   t0, t0, 1     ; epilogue
0x8000_002A          c.addi a0, 1         ; epilogue
0x8000_002C  hold:   jal    x0, hold      ; termination

    A position is the halfword offset of an instruction from the start of its prediction block. The 32-bit instructions take two positions.

    With one prediction slot, an FTB entry holds one branch, so the loops are predicted as several blocks. The accepted p2 slot groups, by block:

    Block start Reached by Branch Position Block end Groups
    0x8000_0008 (otop) O taken I 7 0x8000_0028 15
    0x8000_000E (itop) I taken I 4 0x8000_002E before the split 55
    0x8000_000E (itop) I taken I 4 0x8000_0020 after the split 105
    0x8000_001A I corrected to not taken O 3 0x8000_003A 3
    0x8000_0020 (O) The block at itop ending at O O 0 0x8000_0040 10

    The c.addi and addi instructions do no useful work. Their purpose is to mix 16-bit and 32-bit instructions and to separate the branches.

    The direction of each branch is chosen by the program writer class and resolved by the modeled backend. The register operands play no part in the taken/not taken state. The code is the same on every pass, and only the expected instruction stream changes.

    I is taken 9 times and not taken once per pass, which gives 16 exits with the same trip count. O is taken on passes 0 to 14 and falls through on pass 15 (event 13).

    4. Walkthrough¶

    Cycle numbers below are events in section 5; each event is a marker in the waveform.

    4.1 Predicting the inner exit¶

    The loop predictor allocates an entry when an update misses on a taken backward branch, so I's first taken resolution allocates one, at cyc 20.1 An exit at a known count trains the entry. A count equal to the learned trip count, past_itr, raises the confidence cnf by one, and any other count clears cnf and learns the count. A taken resolution at or past the learned count clears cnf.2

    The LP predicts only at full confidence, 3. It then predicts taken while the speculative count is below the trip count, so the exit is predicted not taken.3 At p1, a trusted LP overrides the direction of a slot the uBTB supplied.4 At p2, it takes priority over TAGE.5 The slot carries pred_src PRED_LOOP in both cases.

    Each of the 16 exits retires with a final slot from one of three predictors, the last group the FTQ accepted for its entry.

    passes   final slot from   direction   result
0 to 3   TAGE              taken       mispredicted; the backend redirects
4 to 7   SC                not taken   correct
8 to 15  loop predictor    not taken   correct

    The exits and the LP's training across the run are shown below.

    The exits of passes 0 to 3 are the backend redirects to 0x8000_001A, from cyc 49 (event 2) to cyc 140 (event 4). The LP is still training over this time: lp_past_itr first reads 8 at cyc 116 (event 3), and lp_conf first reads 1 at cyc 140 and 2 at cyc 199.

    The learned count is 8, not 9 or 10, because the LP indexes its table by the request PC, the start of the prediction block, and not by I's PC. The first iteration of each pass predicts I in the block at otop, which has its own LP entry. The other nine predict it in the block at itop, so that entry sees 8 taken branches and then the exit, at lp_curs 8.6

    In passes 4 to 7, TAGE still predicts I taken at the exit, and SC reverses it at p3. The first reversal is at cyc 162 (event 5) and the last at cyc 238 (event 7). The first redirects to 0x8000_002E, the end of the block at itop. The FTB update at cyc 173 (event 6) ends that entry at O's PC, so the other three redirect to 0x8000_0020.

    lp_conf reaches 3 at cyc 248 (event 8), and the LP supplies I's direction from cyc 256 at p1 (event 9) and cyc 257 at p2 (event 10). It first predicts an exit at cyc 259 (event 11), and the monitor counts the first such exit at retirement at cyc 266 (event 12), in pass 8. Every exit from there on comes from the LP.

    The backend reports 11 mispredictions in the run. Besides the four exits, three are I taken, at cyc 21, 31 and 69, three are O taken, at cyc 59, 171 and 196, and one is O falling through at cyc 429 (event 13).

    4.2 The loop predictor taking over¶

    The waveform group loop_pred_p1_p2 shows the LP's answers for the block at p1 and p2, not the table entry. The p2 answer describes I only in the cycles its block is at p2, and reads 0 otherwise.

    At cyc 116 (event 3), u_bpu.w_lp_pred_p2.[0].lp_past_itr first reads 8, while bpu_slot_p2.[0] is still PRED_TAGE. At cyc 248 (event 8), lp_conf first reads 3.

    At cyc 256 (event 9), u_bpu.w_lp_pred_p1.[0].lp_pred_is_loop is asserted and bpu_pred_slot_p1.[0].pred_src is PRED_LOOP, with u_ftq.w_ok_pred_p1 asserted. At cyc 257 (event 10), u_bpu.w_lp_pred_p2.[0].lp_pred_is_loop is asserted and bpu_slot_p2.[0].pred_src is PRED_LOOP.

    At cyc 259 (event 11), the speculative count lp_curs reads 8, equal to lp_past_itr. lp_pred_taken is deasserted, and bpu_slot_p2.[0] holds I at pos 4 with taken deasserted and pred_src PRED_LOOP.
