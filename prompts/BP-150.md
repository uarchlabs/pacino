## Hypothesis
The p3 successor is right and the defect is in the flush. W3, IB-12,
IB-13 and IBUF-8b all rest on one claim: a p2 or p3 redirect fires
before the entry it names has been fetched. FQ-1 orders the pointers
but does not enforce that claim. When fetch has caught up with
allocation, entry K can be fetched, and its block truncated at the
branch predicted taken at p2, before SC reverses that branch at p3.
The p3 flush at K then does not give back the instructions after the
branch.

## Background
Checked by the PA against the tree at 767eab8. The IA confirms each
fact it uses.
- bp_cluster.sv:1312-1350, p3_redir_comb: a slot SC reverses to not
  taken takes the next slot's successor, ending in r_pft_p3, the block
  fall-through. This is the rule of ftq_bpu_interfaces.md section 6
  (BP-121): "every lower slot's not-taken successor is the next slot's
  successor".
- ftq_ifu_interfaces.md 7 W3: for a p2 or p3 redirect the flush index
  is K, because "K is not yet fetched and including it is required".
- ifu_ibuf_interfaces.md IB-12: the ibuf clears on a backend redirect
  alone; its table gives the reason for p2 and p3 as "fires before the
  named entry is fetched, FQ-1. IBUF-8b". IB-13 repeats W3.
- ibuf_decisions.md IBUF-8b: a p2 or p3 redirect "fires one or two
  cycles after p1", and FQ-1 puts the named entry "at or near
  alloc_ptr. Nothing at or after it has been fetched".
- ftq_decisions.md:446, FQ-1: commit_ptr <= fetch_ptr <= xlate_ptr <=
  alloc_ptr. It orders the pointers; it does not by itself keep
  fetch_ptr from reaching an entry before that entry's p3.
- ftq_ifu.sv:256-258: a request is presented "only for an entry whose
  translation has been presented and whose fetch has not yet issued,
  which is exactly fetch_pending (FQ-1)". Nothing in that comment
  waits for the entry's p3.
- PERF-002, Other Notes 1, with its first backend: 20 of 20 stream
  errors in two 3e5-instruction runs (media_3, web_11) followed an
  SC-predicted conditional, and fetch resumed 4 to 580 bytes past the
  branch's pc + 4; for example web_11 after 0xaaaaae33bf08, fetch
  gave ...bf10 where the path is ...bf0c. A skip of 580 bytes is
  longer than a block, so fetch went to a later slot's target or
  beyond, not only to the fall-through.
- With PERF-002's final backend a skip is counted as a mispredict
  attributed to SC and repaired, so err_stream is 0 and the skip
  inflates SC's mispredict count.
- tb_fe_top programs recur, rvc and misp_call have SC p3 reversals
  (TD#149, BP-121), and no tb_fe_top program has a stream error.
- tools/known_failures.txt: one line per failing target,
  "<makefile dir> <target> TD#? <description>"; regress.sh compares
  failures with it.
