    request the first fetch of the corrected stream.

    ftq_ifu_interfaces.md 5 says entries at or after the flush index

    only when idle. This belongs in TD#134.

    PC and fault, and the instruction complete in the next block.
    IFU-8, DCD-2 and ifu_decisions.md 6 fetch the 17th halfword and

    does not say whether JALR calls or returns count, nor whether a
    JAL before a predicted taken position counts. BUILT: M1 = the
