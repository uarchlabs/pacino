### Phase 1: calls and returns only the backend finds
THE DESIGN.
- The redirect carries bp_br_type_e and the length. The length is
  needed because the push address is the branch's own pc plus its
  length, and the redirect pc is the target.
- Read only on RC_MISPREDICT with _self clear, winning (ftq_rollback_val
  with the backend arm).
- The entry "did not hold" the branch when no slot holds a taken RAS
  operation before the position, or at it (Phase 1: at it as any op).
- The operation goes to the RAS on the C1 ports. The RAS needed no
  change for it. The post-op snapshot is written into the entry
  (w_ras_fix_wr_val), and the entry's commit reads it.
THE INTERACTIONS THE TASK ASKED TO SETTLE:
- The resolution. The resolution of the branch maps or places its slot,
  taken. ftq_resolve's w_occ_wr always writes a taken RAS type, so the
  commit walk commits the branch once (ftq_decisions.md 5.4). The
  speculative operation and the commit are then once each.
- The order. The held judgement reads the entry as the redirect finds
  it. Both drivers present the branch's resolution in the redirect's
  cycle or later (perf_fe.cc 1076-1125; fe_tb_backend.svh 248-250 and
  482). A resolution accepted earlier would show its own slot. It would
  then read as held, no operation would be applied, and the branch
  would reach the RAS at commit only, as before BP-153. Late is safe,
  and never doubled. No ordering rule is placed on the backend.
- The undo (BP-152). A held push after the position is undone first,
  then the operation applies (unchanged). In Phase 1 a held pop after
  the position blocked the operation, as C1 did; Phase 2 replaces that.
- The commit walk. Unchanged.

### Phase 2: the snapshot's off-path pops
THE DESIGN (fields, widths, rules settled here):
- Each p2 snapshot carries the block's pop record. It is taken from the
  state the pop found:
  - pmode (2 bits): NONE, LINK (followed the link), DEC (decremented
    the count, TOSR held) or EMPTY (read the fallback).
  - ptosr (4 bits): TOSR before the pop.
  - ptag (4 bits): the generation at ptosr.
  - +10 bits, 38 in all.
- The p1 snapshot is the block ahead's post-op state, so its record
  would be that block's pop. bp_cluster clears it to NONE.
- ras_rs_undo_pop, on a restore, undoes the record after the push undo
  and before the C1 operation:
  - LINK: TOSR = ptosr, or BOS when the generation moved (B1), with the
    count written 0.
  - DEC: the count at ptosr + 1.
  - EMPTY: nothing on the stack.
  - All: the depth + 1 (the depth half).
  - A record of NONE is not undone.
- ras_pd_snapshot carries the C1 pop's record, else NONE.
- The FTQ raises the undo for a held taken return or return-call after
  the position, or at it as another operation (RC4); a return-call also
  gets the push undo.
- The undo then lets the operation apply; in Phase 1 a held pop blocked
  it.

### The rule changes for acceptance, with the planning text proposed

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 1: THE BACKEND REDIRECT CARRIES THE TYPE.
- Replaces (ftq_backend_interfaces.md 5): the redirect group "val, idx,
  pos, pc, self, cause and taken, with no branch type".
- New rule: two fields are added, bkend_ftq_redir_type (bp_br_type_e,
  3b) and bkend_ftq_redir_rvc (1b), on fe_top and the ftq. Both are the
  resolved type and length of the named instruction. They are read only
  on RC_MISPREDICT with _self clear. A redirect naming no branch drives
  NO_BRANCH.
- Why: the RAS operation of a call or return the backend finds needs
  its type. The push address needs the length, because _pc is the
  target.
- Rejected: requiring the resolution no later than its redirect. It
  puts an ordering rule on the backend, and the resolution still names
  no RAS action. Also rejected: carrying the return address (39 bits)
  instead of _rvc (1 bit).
- Could break: every driver of the redirect group. All are updated:
  perf_top.sv, fe_tb_backend.svh, fe_tb_dut.svh, tb_ftq.
  examples/fe_demo includes fe_tb_dut.svh and fe_tb_backend.svh and is
  not built (FDT-2a). The regression passes.
- Proposed text, ftq_backend_interfaces.md 5, the field table: "_type
  bp_br_type_e: the resolved type of the instruction named, NO_BRANCH
  for one that is not a branch. _rvc: the instruction is a 16-bit
  encoding. Both read only with cause RC_MISPREDICT and _self clear
  (BP-153, RAS-4)."

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 2: THE BACKEND'S RAS OPERATION.
- Replaces (ras_decisions.md 7, BP-152 C1 text): "returns and indirect
  calls the FTB does not give are still applied only at commit (ret_pd,
  call_pd)".
- New rule: "On the restore of a winning RC_MISPREDICT redirect with
  _self clear whose _type is a call, return or return-call, the RAS
  applies the operation to the restored state by the C1 rules (the pop
  first, then the push of the entry pc + 2 * _pos + 2 or 4). This holds
  when the entry holds no taken RAS operation before _pos or at it as
  the same operation. A held operation after _pos, or at it as another,
  is undone first (RAS-4 rule changes 3 and 4). The post-op snapshot is
  written into the entry. The resolution's slot is a taken RAS type and
  always written (ftq_resolve w_occ_wr), so the commit operates once."
- Why: 3,105 residuals (call_pd 704, ret_pd 2,401), all removed.
- Rejected: a separate RAS port for the backend operation. The C1 ports
  already carry exactly this operation on the restore, and one restore
  takes one arm.
- Could break: a double operation when the resolution precedes its
  redirect. The resolved slot then reads as held, and nothing is
  applied (stated in ftq.sv bk_ras). Tests: tb_ftq M1-M10, tb_ras TC-77,
  tb_fe_top ras_bkicall and ras_bkret, and the RAS commit check in every
  tb_fe_top program (regression pass).
- Proposed text:
  - ras_decisions.md 7: the new rule above, as "BUILT BY BP-153, for
    acceptance".
  - 12.14 CB1: "... except the C1 and backend operations, which the FTQ
    forms from a predecode correction or a backend redirect".
  - ras_interfaces.md 4: "ras_pd_* also carry the backend's operation
    (BP-153)".
  - RAS-1: "may be closed: every call and return reaches the RAS
    speculatively".

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 3: THE SNAPSHOT'S POP RECORD AND THE UNDO OF
AN OFF-PATH POP.
- Replaces (ras_decisions.md 4.3, BP-152 rule change 4): "A pop cannot
  be undone from a post-op snapshot (no pre-op TOSR): left,
  snap_offpath_op."
- New rule:
  - "bp_ras_snapshot_t carries the block's pop record: pmode (2b: NONE,
    LINK, DEC, EMPTY), ptosr (RAS_PTR_BITS), ptag (RAS_TAG_BITS), from
    the state the pop found. The p1 snapshot carries NONE.
    ras_pd_snapshot carries the C1 pop's record, else NONE."
  - "With ras_rs_undo_pop the restore undoes it after the push undo,
    before the C1 operation, as RP4/RP5 do. LINK: TOSR = ptosr unless
    the generation at ptosr is not ptag (then BOS), and that entry's
    count 0. DEC: the count at ptosr + 1 when ptosr is the restored top.
    EMPTY: no stack change. The depth + 1 in every case. NONE: nothing."
  - "The FTQ raises it for a held taken return or return-call after the
    redirect position, or at it as another operation, on both arms."
- Why: snap_offpath_op 988 (1,039 after Phase 1), all removed.
- Rejected: the depth half alone (dsp + 1 with no field). It leaves the
  pop's pointer, so the next pop reads one frame too deep.
- Could break: an undo whose record is not the block's own. The p1
  snapshot is cleared for that reason. A p3-repaired block's slots and
  record can disagree (TD#149): the record is NONE when p2 made no pop.
  Tests: tb_ras TC-78a-e, the tb_ras model (214,258 and 172,487
  comparisons, 0 mismatches), ras_assert.sv depth, tb_ftq M11-M18,
  tb_fe_top ras_offpop.
- Proposed text:
  - ras_decisions.md 4.1, 4.3 (replacing the sentence above), 9, 12.9
    new RE10: the rule above.
  - ras_interfaces.md 4: input ras_rs_undo_pop, read only with
    ras_restore_val.
  - ras_interfaces.md 5, bp_ras_snapshot_t: "+ pmode 2b, ptosr 4b, ptag
    4b: 38b".
  - bp_cluster port ras_rs_undo_pop.
  - ftq_ifu_interfaces.md 7: "pd_ras raises ras_pd_undo_pop for a held
    pop the correction leaves off the path".

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 4: A HELD OPERATION AT THE POSITION.
- Replaces:
  - ftq_ifu pd_ras (BP-152): a held push at mis_pos "no longer a call"
    is off the path; a held pop is never undone and blocks C1.
  - BP-153 Phase 1's bk_ras: any held operation at _pos blocks.
- New rule: "A held operation at the position is kept when the corrected
  (predecode) or resolved (RC_MISPREDICT) operation is the same push,
  pop or both (a direct and an indirect call alike). Otherwise each of
  its parts is undone and the new operation applies. For a backend cause
  other than RC_MISPREDICT the held operation at _pos is kept: no type
  names it."
- Why: one rule for push and pop, and for both arms. A return the FTB
  typed as a call at the same PC, or the reverse, is corrected.
- Rejected: matching the exact type. It would undo and redo a call push
  for a direct/indirect difference with the same effect.
- Could break: BP-152's C1 cases. TC-75/76, tb_ftq_ifu (237/0 and
  222/0) and the tb_fe_top programs pass. Tests: tb_ftq M8, M14-M16,
  M18.
- Proposed text: ras_decisions.md 4.3 and 7, as the rule above.

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 5: THE WIDTHS.
- Replaces: ras_interfaces.md 5 and ras_decisions.md 12.13 PM2, the
  snapshot at 28 bits; ftq_entry_formats.md 1 and ftq_decisions.md 1,
  the entry at 316 bits (two slots) and 241 (one).
- New rule: bp_ras_snapshot_t is 41 bits, 4 * RAS_PTR_BITS (5) +
  RAS_RCTR_WIDTH (4) + RAS_DEPTH_BITS (8) + 2 * RAS_TAG_BITS (3) +
  RAS_PMODE_BITS (2) + 1. bp_ftq_entry_t is 329 bits at two slots and
  254 at one; 64 entries, 21,056 bits at two slots.
- Why: RC3 (push, pmode, ptosr, ptag) and RC11 (5-bit pointers, 3-bit
  tags).
- Rejected: storing the pop record in a side array indexed by FTQ entry.
  It is written and read with the snapshot, so a separate array only
  adds a port.
- Could break: every literal of the snapshot. Tests: tb_ras TC-64,
  tb_bp_pkg, tb_bp_cluster CB4 (cb4_snap), tb_ftq_entry J4/J5, all
  passing; the regression passes.
- Proposed text: as the new rule, in ras_interfaces.md 5, ras_decisions.md
  12.13 PM2, ftq_entry_formats.md 1 and ftq_decisions.md 1 ("BP-153,
  RAS-4").

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 6: THE RETURN TARGET IS THE PEEK.
- Replaces (bp_cluster, ras_interfaces.md 4): the p2 target of a RETURN
  or RETURN_CALL slot is ras_pop_addr_p2, valid only for a slot that
  pops.
- New rule: "The RAS gives, per slot, the address the slot's pop would
  read (ras_peek_addr_p2, ras_peek_valid_p2), whether it pops or not.
  The p2 target of a return or return-call slot is the peek. For a slot
  that pops, the peek is the pop."
- Why: p3_reach 403 and snap_repair 37, both removed. A return beyond an
  earlier taken slot made no p2 pop and took the FTB target. When p3
  turned that slot not taken, that target was the return's successor.
- Rejected: holding the p3 successor for the repair's pop address. That
  adds a p3 lookup of the RAS after the repair, a stage later.
- Could break: a slot that pops reads a different address. Tests: tb_ras
  TC-79 (the peek equals the pop for a popping slot, and the would-be
  pop otherwise, checked against the model); tb_bp_cluster CB11.
- Proposed text: ras_interfaces.md 4, outputs ras_peek_addr_p2 and
  ras_peek_valid_p2 as above; ras_decisions.md 12.14 CB2: "the return
  target is the peek (BP-153)".

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 7: A HELD OPERATION IS ONE THE SNAPSHOT
RECORDS.
- Replaces (BP-152 pd_ras, BP-153 RC2/RC4): an entry's taken call or
  return slot is a held RAS operation.
- New rule: "A taken call slot is a held push only when the entry's
  snapshot has push set; a taken return slot is a held pop only when its
  pmode is not NONE. A slot the RAS did not operate (a p1 uBTB slot no
  p2 slot replaced) is neither held nor undone." In bk_ras and pd_ras.
- Why: bos_rewind 249 -> 15, commit_lost 36 -> 20, extra_deep -> 0. The
  undo of a push never made retracted another block's frame.
- Rejected: clearing such slots from the entry. The slot is the p1
  prediction the path took and is needed by the FTB update and the
  commit.
- Could break: an operation the snapshot does not record and the RAS did
  make. The snapshot is post-op and both bits come from the same scan.
  Tests: tb_ftq M19-M21 (fail with the gating off), tb_fe_top and the
  regression.
- Proposed text: ras_decisions.md 4.3 and 7: the new rule above;
  ras_interfaces.md 5: "push: the block pushed".

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 8: THE P3 BLOCK'S SNAPSHOT AFTER THE REPAIR

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 9: ON AN FTB MISS THE RAS OPERATES ON THE P1

THE ITTAGE P2 READY (found in Phase 4; a defect against the written
rule, not a rule change).
- The rule (bp_arb_spec.md 4.6): "At p1/u1: trx_type==PRED: RAM read
  result flows to result formation. trx_type==UPD: RAM write completes.
  No result routing."
- The defect: ittage_cntrl.sv meta_out read "ittage_pred_rdy_p2 =
  trx_type ? '0 : rdy_p2_r". rdy_p2_r and meta_p2_r are the registered
  result of the PRED transaction of the cycle before; trx_type is the
  arbiter's combinational grant of the current cycle (ittage.sv:196),
  the NEXT transaction. So a finished prediction was dropped whenever an
  update was granted in its p2 cycle, and bp_cluster.sv:1224 took the
  FTB jump target. The arbiter grants a prediction or an update, never
  both, and predictions first (ittage_arb.sv:183), so an update is
  granted in exactly the cycles after a prediction with none behind it.
  tage_cntrl gates at entry (tage_cntrl.sv:311), not at the result.
- The fix: ittage_pred_rdy_p2 = rdy_p2_r.
- Why: the mpki_ftb rises of BP-152 and of this task (Phase 4), and 0.8
  to 0.9 MPKI on infra_1 and web_11 (p5 below).
- Rejected: gating with the registered transaction type (arb_trx_r).
  It is PRED whenever rdy_p2_r is set, so it adds nothing.
- Could break: a consumer that relied on the result being withheld in
  an update cycle. bp_cluster reads ITTAGE only at p2 with its own
  branch_id check; ITTAGE aging already counted rdy_p2_r unmasked.
  Tests: tb_ittage_cntrl TC-PRED-07 (fails before at both slot counts:
  "FAIL PRED07 rdy with an update granted at p2: exp=1 act=0", 149/1
  and 140/1; passes after, 150/0 and 141/0), tb_ittage (258 and 389
  checks), tb_bp_cluster 2126/0 and 1543/0, tb_fe_top, the regression.
- Proposed text: none required; bp_arb_spec.md 4.6 already states it.
  For ittage_interfaces.md, if it lists ittage_pred_rdy_p2: "the p2
  result of the prediction granted the cycle before, whatever the grant

RULE CHANGE FOR ACCEPTANCE 13: THE FTB JUMP FIELD HOLDS THE FULL VA
DISPLACEMENT.
- Replaces (ftb_decisions.md 4.2, ruled session-052): "A J-type reaches
  +/-1 MB original, +/-2 MB expanded, so FTB_JMP_TGT_BITS = 21. Both pair
  with TAR_STAT_BITS = 2."
- New rule: "FTB_JMP_TGT_BITS = VA_WIDTH (59): the jump field's
  displacement, from the jump PC, reconstructs every target exactly.
  The jump field is the target of every jump type, and 4.2 makes it the
  fallback for a JALR on an ITTAGE miss; a JALR target is anywhere in
  the VA. The uBTB's jump field stays at the J-type reach (21,
  UBTB_JMP_TGT_BITS no longer follows FTB_JMP_TGT_BITS): its target is
  the p1 prediction, which p2 corrects with a redirect, not a flush."
- Why (Phase 4): at 21 bits every JALR more than 2**20 bytes from its
  jump PC got a wrong FTB target; ftb_cntrl wrote its status (jmp_stat)
  and the lookup did not read it. After the ITTAGE fix, 5,454 mispredicts
  over the six p5 traces (immediate) were FTB-sourced out-of-reach
  indirects, 5,317 of them to the PC's last target (web_11 4,557,
  infra_1 682). Measured after the change: p5 below.
- Cost: the FTB entry 114 -> 152 bits (RAM entry 113 -> 151), 2,048
  entries, 231,424 -> 309,248 RAM bits (+77,824, +33.6%). The uBTB does
  not change.
- Rejected:
  - the jump field gives no target when its status is not fit: the
    fallback is then wrong anyway, so no mispredict is saved;
  - Xiangshan-style upper bits from the PC +/- 1 at the reach
    granularity: these targets are 2**34 away, still out of reach;
