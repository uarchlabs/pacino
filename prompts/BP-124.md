## Background
BP-123 built the walker, the pacino L1I copy and the IFU's
maintenance and uncached paths, and passed tools/regress.sh with 149
targets. Its results raised three things Jeff ruled on:

- MMU-30 returned a guest physical address above bit 40 as 0. That
  collides with a kind-00 fault on guest page 0, whose GPA is also 0,
  so htval would lose the offset (BP-123 risk 1). A VS-stage PTE holds
  a 44-bit PPN, so a walk can form a 56-bit GPA; Shtvala requires
  htval to carry it.
- Under Svadu the walker set A on a single-stage or VS-stage leaf
  before the ITLB checked U against the privilege, so a fetch that
  then faulted on U could leave A set (BP-123 risk 2, D8). The
  privileged translation algorithm makes every leaf check (steps 6 to
  8) before the A/D update (step 9).
- The decoder mapped FP, AMO and CSR instructions onto placeholder
  operations before BP-123, and BP-123 (D4) mapped the SYSTEM fences,
  HLV/HLVX/HSV, WRS and FLH/FSH onto the representation already used
  for their class, so a backend could not tell those operations
  apart (risk 3). Reading the decoder in session-077 also found
  reserved encodings in those spaces accepted, which RVA23 recommends
  raising illegal.

BP-123's D1, D2, D3, D6 and D7 were accepted as built and are in the
planning documents. D2's access-type port was built on the walker
only and tied to execute in tb_fe_top; Problem 2 has the ITLB drive
it.

## Binding Previous Decisions
- GPA_WIDTH stays 41 everywhere except the faulting GPA (MMU-23).
  The ITLB entry, the IFU's translation result and every address that
  translates keep their widths.
- The walker performs one walk at a time and the ITLB tracker stays
  at depth 1 (MMU-U2, ITLB-15).
- htval and htinst are not built; there is no CSR file. The GPA and
  its kind end at ifu_pd_pkt_t (MMU-29).
- ITLB-16 stands: the ITLB still checks U on every hit. The walker's
  check is in addition (MMU-8c).
- G-stage permissions stay as MMU-26 says: every G-stage access is a
  user access, checked by the walker, cause 20.
- C and Zcb stay always enabled (DCD-20). A slot carrying a fetch
  fault is not decoded as illegal (DCD-19).
- tools/known_failures.txt is not edited (CLAUDE.md).
- A design choice not settled by this task or the planning set is
  put to Jeff in session. If he is not there to answer, the IA takes
  its recommended option, builds it, records it in Results Capture as
  provisional, and continues; Jeff confirms or reverses after

Problem 1. FAULT_GPA_WIDTH (Part A).
  Done when:
  - bp_defines_pkg declares FAULT_GPA_WIDTH = 56, in the form GPA_WIDTH
    is declared, beside it;
  - l2t_itlb_gpa and itlb_ifu_gpa are [FAULT_GPA_WIDTH-1:0] on the
    walker, the ITLB, the IFU and fe_top, and so are the GPA in the
    ITLB's held fault, in both results of the IFU translation queue
    entry, and ifu_pd_pkt_t.fault_gpa;
  - the walker returns every faulting GPA whole: for kind 00 the
    faulting page with bits [11:0] zero, every higher bit formed as
    the translation algorithm forms it; for kinds 01 and 10 the guest
    physical address of the VS-stage PTE;
  - MMU-30's logic is gone from the walker and the IFU: no GPA is
    replaced by 0, and the IFU fills fault_gpa[11:0] from the faulting
    halfword's virtual address for every kind-00 fault;
  - tb_ptw checks a GPA with bit 55 set, at a VS-stage leaf (kind 00)
    and at a VS-stage non-leaf (kind 01);
  - tb_fe_top's program pt_faults_h checks at ifu_pd_pkt_t: its two
    GPA-above-bit-40 cases, each now the whole address; and a new
    case, a kind-00 guest-page fault on guest page 0 at a non-zero
    page offset, whose fault_gpa is that offset.

Problem 2. The walker's leaf checks and the request (Part B).
  Done when:
  - the client group carries itlb_l2t_acc [1:0] and itlb_l2t_priv
    [1:0], ITLB to walker, on the ITLB, the walker, fe_top and
    tb_fe_top; the ITLB drives itlb_l2t_acc with 2 (execute) and
    itlb_l2t_priv with its current privilege from its CSR input group
    (ITLB-17), 2'b00 U or 2'b01 S, and V on itlb_l2t_v as today;
    tb_fe_top's tie of itlb_l2t_acc is removed;
  - before any A or D update on a leaf the walker has made every check
    MMU-8c names on that leaf: the reserved encodings (MMU-U6,
    MMU-U7), the permissions of MMU-26a, the misaligned superpage, and
    for a single-stage or VS-stage leaf U against itlb_l2t_priv and
    itlb_l2t_v (a fetch at U or VU needs U set, a fetch at S or VS
    needs U clear, a read or write is checked with SUM clear). On a
    failure it returns the fault, with the cause MMU-24c gives for
    the access type, and writes nothing to memory;
  - in ptw_assert, a property fires if the walker issues a CAS that
    sets A or D on a leaf any of whose MMU-8c checks fails, and an
    assertion fires if an accepted request presents itlb_l2t_priv
    2'b10 or 2'b11;
  - every fault the ITLB holds (ITLB-10a) records the privilege and V
    of the request that raised it; a re-request whose privilege or V
    differs drops the held fault and issues a new walk at the current
    privilege; one that matches is answered with the held fault
    (ITLB-10b);
  - tb_ptw covers, under Svadu with A clear: a fetch at U from a leaf
    with U clear, a fetch at S from a leaf with U set, and the same two
    at VU and VS through the G-stage, each returning cause 12 with the
    PTE unchanged in the memory model; a read and a store at S against
    a leaf with U set, cause 13 and 15 respectively, with the PTE
    unchanged; and for each other MMU-8c check, at each stage where
    it applies (single-stage, VS-stage and G-stage leaves), one case
    where it fails on a leaf with A clear and the PTE stays
    unchanged;
  - tb_itlb covers a held fault re-requested at the same privilege
    (answered from the hold) and at a different one (a new walk);
  - tb_fe_top has a new program pt_upriv, target sim_fe_top_pt_upriv
    in REGRESS_TARGETS, in which under Svadu a fetch at U faults on a
    U-clear page and a fetch at S faults on a U-set page, each with A
    clear, the trap taken and A still clear in memory afterwards, and
    one fetch at S walks after a fault held from U.

Problem 3. The decode representation and legality (Part C).
  Done when instr_decoder and decode_pkg meet dcd_decisions.md DCD-21
  to DCD-35 in full, and in particular:

    ALU_FLOAD, ALU_FSTORE and ALU_CSRRW with their values; the 79

    the values 107 to 185; every vector instruction has ALU_VEC;

    fp_fmt [1:0], fp_src_fmt [1:0], rd_is_fp, rs1_is_fp, rs2_is_fp,
    is_hlsv, is_hlvx; encodings and meanings as DCD-23, DCD-24 and
    DCD-30 state;
  - uses_rd, uses_rs1, uses_rs2 and uses_rs3 are exact per operation,

  - legality as DCD-26 to DCD-32, each extension's instructions gated
    by the enable DCD-26 names;
  - Zvbb is decoded as DCD-33 states: the ten v_op_class_t values

  - every reader of alu_op_t, decode_pkt_t or v_op_class_t under rtl/

Problem 4. The oracle sweep and the reports.
  Done when:
  - the decode Makefile has two targets, both in REGRESS_TARGETS:
    - sim_dec_sweep: the decoder compared against the oracle file
      rtl/core/frontend/decode/tb/dec_llvm_oracle.txt;
    - oracle_dec_sweep: rtl/core/frontend/decode/tb/
      gen_dec_llvm_oracle.py regenerates the oracle into the build
      directory and compares it with the committed file;
  - the oracle file has one line per swept encoding: the 32-bit word
    as 8 lower-case hex digits, one space, then llvm-objdump's
    disassembly text, or <unknown> where it decodes none;
  - the sweep covers every value of every funct, fmt, rm, width and
    selector field of OP_FP, the four FMA opcodes, AMO, MISC_MEM
    (the whole 12-bit immediate), SYSTEM (the whole funct12),
    OP_VECTOR and the vector forms of LOAD-FP and STORE-FP (DCD-34).
    Register fields take the values 0 and 1 in every combination,
    except a register field that selects an operation for some
    instruction of that opcode, which also takes all 32 values with
    every other register field at 1: rs1 and rs2 in OP_FP, vs1/rs1
    in OP_VECTOR, and rs2 (lumop, sumop) in the vector forms of
    LOAD-FP and STORE-FP;
  - for each encoding the sweep compares LLVM's legality with
    decode's is_illegal, and LLVM's mnemonic with decode's alu_op or
    v_op_class through a table from each LLVM mnemonic to the
    operation DCD-22, DCD-29 and DCD-33 assign its encoding (an
    alias such as fence.tso, pause or prefetch.i maps to the
    operation of its encoding). The table is derived from the
    specification, not chosen, and is listed in Results Capture;
  - the disassembler is the llvm-objdump that gen_ifu_rvc_exp_oracle.py
    runs (LLVM 21, the embecosm toolchain, PROJECT_STATUS session-075),
    with --mattr exactly
    +m,+a,+f,+d,+c,+v,+zicsr,+zifencei,+zicond,+zimop,+zcmop,+zcb,
    +zfa,+zfhmin,+zawrs,+zba,+zbb,+zbs,+zicbom,+zicbop,+zicboz,
    +zihintntl,+zihintpause,+zvbb,+zvfhmin,+h,+svinval.
    If llvm-objdump prints any warning naming one of these features,
    that is a design choice, handled as Binding Previous Decisions
    says;
  - every disagreement is resolved against the ratified specification
    and listed with the encoding, LLVM's answer, decode's answer, the
    specification text and the outcome. Where the specification does
    not settle it, decode is not changed and the encoding is listed
    for Jeff (DCD-32);
  - Results Capture reports, from the RTL, for a writeback with
    ifu_ftq_uc_end set (W3U): what W1 and W2 of ftq_ifu_interfaces.md
    7 write for the slot at mis_pos, the flush index F the FTQ uses,
    and which FTQ leaf, ftq_ifu or ftq_npc, forms the W3U successor
    (ftq_decisions.md 4.2 and 7.5). This is a report; nothing
    changes.
