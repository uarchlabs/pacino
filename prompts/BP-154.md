## Hypothesis
At 16 entries the wrap returns at about BP-153's count before RC11
(wrap 8, fb_uncommitted 1 over the 25 runs). At long resolve to
commit (50 to 200 cycles) the wrap grows, because more calls are in
flight, until the FTQ's 64 entries bound them. pt_gonly_x4's move
comes from one of BP-153's changes.

  (Makefile:126) to the classifier.

  uncommitted call A, 20 call-return pairs, then A's return; at 16
  entries the 16th allocation reuses A's entry. TC-64 checks the

  (tb_bp_cluster.sv about 587) builds a snapshot.
- BP-153 RC11 removed wrap 8 and fb_uncommitted 1 (1,064 -> 1,059 over
