## Background
BP-126 reported (PD-17) that sc_idx_hash's formula shifts inp_pc_p2,
which is PC[VA_WIDTH-1:1], by PC_HASH_SHIFT = 2, so the index starts
at PC[3], while its prose says the shift drops the instruction
offset. Jeff ruled for the prose. inp_pc_p2 is the prediction block
start, the same value on both slots (ftq_bpu_interfaces.md 3); the
two branches of a block are separated by the per-slot RAMs.

{9, 9, 9, 9, 10}, and a comment saying TAGE, ITTAGE and SC all form
(pc >> PC_HASH_SHIFT); sc_table.sv forms the index as the formula
above.
