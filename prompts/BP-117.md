## Background
C6. ftq_decisions.md 5.5 R1 gives the flush index F by source. The
predecode row is F = K+1: K is already fetched, its positions up to
mis_pos are in the ibuf (ifu_ibuf_interfaces.md IB-2), the ibuf does
not clear on a predecode redirect (IB-12), and refetching K delivers
them twice. p2 and p3 stay at F = K. BP-112 built the predecode arm
at K with p2 and p3. The cause bus cannot carry the distinction:
ftq_npc drives RC_MISPREDICT with _self clear for p2, p3, predecode
and the backend alike. BP-112 routed ftq_npc's arm_win to ftq_ifu
and ftq_ptr for this; arms 2, 3 and 4 are predecode, p3 and p2
(5.5 R1, ftq_ifu_interfaces.md 7 W3).

C5. ftq_ifu_interfaces.md 6 M1: predecode found a JAL, call or not,
before the predicted taken position, or anywhere when no taken
position is predicted. M1 names the first such JAL. A JALR is never
M1. The session-074 record has the built IFU firing M1 only when no
taken position is predicted.

C19. itlb_ifu_interfaces.md IT-16: ifu_itlb_vpn is
[VA_WIDTH-13:0], VA_WIDTH - 12 = 29 bits, not VPN_WIDTH (27). With
V=1 and vsatp.MODE=Bare the fetch PC is a 41-bit guest physical
address; with V=0 and satp.MODE=Bare a fetch address above bit 35
must reach the PMA check to fault. A 27-bit port drops both.

ifu_rvc_exp. BP-116 built it and tb_ifu_rvc_exp with a reference
model written from the specification's field tables. That reference
found decode's rvc_expander wrong in six classes, among them
negative C.BEQZ/C.BNEZ offsets, c.mul as MULW and the doubleword
store offsets. The same reference is the only check on ifu_rvc_exp.
C, Zcb and Zcmop are mandatory in RVA23.

### Problem 4: the oracle
Source: LLVM 21.0.0git, llvm-objdump and clang from the embecosm
toolchain installed at /usr/local/riscv-embecosm-embedded-ubuntu2204-
20250309/bin. Both binaries ship in that toolchain.

Method: all 49,152 encodings are written to one ELF .text. llvm-objdump
--mattr=+c,+m,+f,+d,+zba,+zbb,+zcb,+zcmop disassembles them. LLVM's
RISC-V instruction printer uncompresses each RVC instruction through
LLVM's own compress-pattern table before printing, so the printed
text is LLVM's 32-bit expansion. clang (-march=rv64imafd_zba_zbb,
.option norvc, -mno-relax) re-encodes that text. The generator checks
that each line became exactly one 32-bit word with bits [1:0] = 11.

Why it is independent: the expansion table, immediate scrambles, field
placement and opcodes all come from LLVM's TableGen definitions. The
generator does no field arithmetic for class 1. It shares no code or
tables with ifu_rvc_exp, decode's rvc_expander or the tb's spec_*
corrections, and was not written from them.

  TOOLS-002 was not needed.
- tools/riscv-opcodes: gives match/mask and immediate bit-order
  strings, but no expansion mapping. c_uimm1/c_uimm2 (Zcb) have no
  bit-order entry. Not used.
- Toolchains under ~/reynaldo and ~/reynaldo_mnt were not used. They
  are file systems of other machines mounted here, not toolchains
  installed on this machine.

## RVA23 compliance risks and gaps noticed
- C.MV: LLVM's canonical expansion is addi rd, rs2, 0 and the spec's
  is add rd, x0, rs2. ifu_rvc_exp follows the spec. Both are
  architecturally equivalent. Decode must accept either word.
- C.LUI nzimm=0 (rd not x2, not C.MOP): LLVM accepts it as lui rd,0.
  The spec reserves it, and ifu_rvc_exp raises illegal. If test
  programs built with LLVM ever emit it, a spec-correct core traps.
- Zicfiss is not enabled in the oracle. Its c.sspush x1 and
  c.sspopchk x5 sit on c.mop.1 and c.mop.5, which the IFU expands to
  NOP. Under RVA23 that is correct while Zicfiss is not implemented.
  If Zicfiss is ever added, those two expansions must change.
- Zihintntl (RVA23 mandatory): C.NTL.* are C.ADD rd=x0 HINTs. They

- oracle_ifu_rvc_exp needs the toolchain at RVC_ORACLE_TC. On a
