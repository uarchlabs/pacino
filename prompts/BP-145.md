# Task Overview
Pacino now supports Sv39, Sv48 and Sv57 and their x4 G-stage modes, in
one build (Jeff, session-085; mmu_decisions.md MMU-20). This is the
first of three tasks that build it. It does the widths only:
1. Every address width in the RTL and its testbenches follows the
   package parameters, and every Sv39 or Sv39x4 bound becomes a named
   constant of its own. At today's widths the regression gives the
   same result as BP-144's run.
2. The tree moves to the Sv57 widths: VA_WIDTH 59, GPA_WIDTH 59,
   FAULT_GPA_WIDTH 59, full-width predicted targets (FE-19, MMU-23).
   The walker still walks Sv39 and Sv39x4 only, and the ITLB still
   checks Sv39 only. Their behavior at 59 bits is the same as at 41,
   including in the cases that 59 bits makes reachable.
3. Every PC of every CBP2025 training trace is checked against Sv57
   (perf_decisions.md PF-16).

The next task builds the walker's modes, Sv48, Sv57, Sv48x4 and Sv57x4.
The one after builds the ITLB's page sizes and mode checks and the
harness page tables.
