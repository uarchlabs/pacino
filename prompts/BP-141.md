### Problem 8: en_zvfhmin -- the decoder cannot gate it, no change
Zvfhmin adds vfwcvt.f.f.v and vfncvt.f.f.w at SEW = 16. The same two
encodings (OPFVV funct6 0x12, vs1 0x0c and 0x14,
instr_decoder.sv:505-509) are V's conversions at SEW = 32 (and Zvfh's
at SEW = 16). Nothing in the instruction word gives SEW; it is
vtype.vsew, and the decoder holds no vtype (it is stateless and marks
needs_vtype; rename resolves vtype, CLAUDE.md). So decode cannot tell a
Zvfhmin instruction from a V one. The gating belongs where vtype is
resolved for the instruction: at rename/dispatch, or the vector unit's
illegal-instruction check at issue. The check there: VOP_VFWCVT_FF or
VOP_VFNCVT_FF with vsew = e16 and en_zvfhmin = 0 is illegal. The same
point already has to make the other SEW = 16 FP vector forms illegal,
since Zvfh is not implemented.

### Problem 9: TD#194 -- the count is NOT restored
