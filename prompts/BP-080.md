## Hypothesis
The tage break is entirely a set of dangling references to three
symbols removed from bp_structs_pkg.sv (cond_pred_meta_t,
cond_pred_upd_inp_t, tage_high_conf). The referenced logic is
vestigial -- cond_pred_* belonged to the retired merged TAGE+SC
metadata model (TD#94); tage_high_conf was a superseded confidence
field (TD#95). Removing or replacing the dangling references restores
elaboration with no live-functionality loss, and the existing tage
suite passes once they are gone. The exact fix for tage_high_conf
(delete vs replace with the TD#87 strong/medium/weak generation) is a
scope decision for the user, which is why this task reports before
editing.
