## Decisions made not explicit in the prompt
All are provisional under decision 5, item 4 of session-075: Jeff was
not present, so the recommended option was built and recorded here.
- D1. The history bundle stays 2 wide at both values. This covers
  bp_cluster to bp_history (pred_taken [1:0], pred_pc [2],
  num_branches) and the FTQ rollback ports ftq_rollback_n / tkn / pbit
  [1:0].
  - At 1 at most one branch is reported; the upper bit is 0.
  - Alternative: size them by NUM_PRED_SLOTS. That would change
    bp_history, ftq_npc and the cluster/ftq ports, which is more
    surface for no function at 1.
- D2. The FTB and uBTB entry formats keep two conditional fields plus
  the jump field at 1. Slot 0 takes br0, or the jump when br0 is empty;
  br1 and a jump behind br0 are not predicted at 1, and the backend
  corrects them.
  - This is the existing cluster and uBTB rule (interfaces 5.1;
    ubtb.sv "At NUM_PRED_SLOTS = 1 only the br0 field is reported").
  - The tb_fe_top cycle costs in C come from it.
- D3. ubtb keeps a slot parameter, renamed N_SLOTS, with its default
  taken from the package. tb_ubtb builds one uBTB at 2 and one at 1 in
  every build, so both widths are tested at both package values.
  Every other module lost its slot parameter.
- D4. tb_fe_top loops, at 1, skips its two loop-predictor end checks.
  - Mechanism: the inner loop branch (+0x18) and the always-taken
    conditional after it (+0x1c) are in one block. At 1 the +0x1c
    branch has no slot. It mispredicts on every outer iteration, and
    ftq_resolve places its resolution in the last slot (BP-119 rule),
    which is slot 0, the loop branch's.
  - Observed: unmapped resolutions 11 at 1, 6 at 2. The LP supplied 0
    p2 directions. +0x18 mispredicted 3 times after warm-up.
  - Inferred, not traced: the LP entry for +0x18 never becomes trusted
    because the displaced resolution trains it.
  - The lp program, whose loop branch is alone in its block, keeps both
    checks at 1 and passes them, so the loop predictor works at 1.
  - Not taken: changing the placement rule. That would change 2-slot
    behaviour and a session ruling (BP-119).
- D5. ftq assert_labels_1slot skips 3 E5 to E7 labels (a_ras_needs_step,
  a_ras_type, a_ras_snapshot_src) in the tb_ftq_ifu model only.
  - tb_ftq_ifu ties commit_step_val to 0, so they cannot fire there at
    either value; at 1 Verilator folds them away.
  - They stay checked in the tb_ftq_entry and tb_ftq models at 1.
- D6. The 1-slot object directories are $(VER_OUT)_1slot*, matched by
  each Makefile's clean (bpu clean extended; ftq and fe_top clean
  already remove $(VER_OUT)*).
