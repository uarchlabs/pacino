"2. The program", "3. Walkthrough", "4. Events", with an optional "5.
Notes". Today only docs/fd09_tage.md matches. The other 20 have "1. The
program ... 5. Waveform" and get no markers.

Reading the table (mk_sucl.py events()): the lines starting with "|"
between "## 4. Events" and the next level-2 heading. The first row is
the header, and its column 0 must be "cyc". The second row is the
separator. Every row has 3 cells and ends in "|". A row with a decimal
cyc starts an event; a row with an empty cyc adds its text to the event
above. Marker name: c<cyc>_<label>. The label is the event text with
backquotes removed, up to its first ';', ':', ',' or '(', characters
outside [A-Za-z0-9_] removed, at most 6 words joined by '_'. Marker
time: the first FST time at which tb.cyc equals cyc with tb.rstn high,
read from that scenario's own out/<s>/<s>.fst. The markers go after
the groups, then item_unfocus and zoom_to <t0 - 2 cycles> <t0 + 30
cycles>, where t0 is the first marker.
