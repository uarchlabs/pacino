## Context Comments
INST_OFFSET is declared once in bp_defines_pkg and read by two
groups of consumers that have nothing to do with each other.

Group 1, granularity. ftb_cntrl and ubtb use it as the shift that
reduces a byte offset to the stored partial fall-through index and
reconstructs the address from it. Here it is log2 of the byte size
of one in-block position slot and it is tied to FTB_BR_POS_BITS.

Group 2, hashing. tage_table, tage_bim, ittage_table and sc_table
use it as the count of low PC bits discarded before an index or tag
hash. Here it is a hash tuning parameter with no relationship to
the position width.

The two must move independently. If FTB_BR_POS_BITS is ever raised
to address 2-byte RVC positions, group 1 must follow and group 2
must not, because changing group 2 rehashes every table and
invalidates all stored state and all tuning.

tage_table.sv additionally declares its own local INST_OFFSET = 2,
shadowing the package, so a change to the package value would
silently miss that module.

## Hypothesis
The two meanings can be separated by a mechanical rename with no
behavioural change, because both quantities are 2 at the shipped
geometry. Every sim and lint target should reproduce its existing
count exactly. Any count that moves indicates a site classified
into the wrong group.

## Background
Raised while specifying the FTQ to IFU interface. Section 10 of
ftq_ifu_interfaces.md records that FTB_BR_POS_BITS = 3 gives 4-byte
position granularity, so two RVC branches in one aligned word share
a position under RVA23, which mandates the C extension. Costing the
fix showed that the widening is cheap in storage and RTL but is
blocked by INST_OFFSET carrying both meanings. This task removes
that blocker. It does NOT change the position width.

  serves.

## RVA23 compliance risks and gaps noticed

- PC_HASH_SHIFT = 2 discards PC bit 1. RVA23 mandates the C
  extension, so bit 1 distinguishes two RVC instructions and the
  two hash to the same index. Whether 1 predicts better is
  unmeasured and nothing in the repository can measure it. Recorded
  in the package comment. NOT changed here: changing it rehashes
  every table.
- FTB_BR_POS_BITS = 3 gives 4-byte position granularity, so two RVC
  branches in one aligned word share a position and the predictors
  train them as one branch. Recorded in ftq_ifu_interfaces.md
  section 10 with the measured cost of the fix: 9,472 bits, about 3
  percent, plus roughly 190 hardcoded 3-bit position literals in
  tb_ftb, tb_ubtb and tb_bp_cluster, and the bp_history path-bit
  identity moving from pos[1] ^ pos[0] to pos[2] ^ pos[1].
  Unchanged by this task.
